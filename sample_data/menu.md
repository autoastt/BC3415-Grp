# Blaze Burger Co. Menu

All prices are in Singapore dollars (SGD) and include GST. Prices are the same at every Blaze Burger Co. outlet.

## Burgers

The Classic Blaze: a 150 g grass-fed beef patty, cheddar, lettuce, tomato, pickles and Blaze sauce on a toasted brioche bun. Price: $9.90.

Double Inferno: two beef patties, pepper jack cheese, jalapeños, chipotle mayo and crispy onions. Spice level: hot. Price: $14.50.

Smoky Bacon Stack: beef patty, streaky bacon, smoked cheddar, caramelised onions and BBQ sauce. Price: $12.90.

Crispy Chicken Flame: buttermilk fried chicken thigh, slaw, honey sriracha and pickles. Price: $10.90.

Garden Ember (vegetarian): a chickpea and black bean patty, avocado, rocket, tomato and herb yoghurt. Price: $10.50. Can be made vegan by swapping the yoghurt for vegan aioli and the brioche for a sourdough bun at no extra cost.

Little Blaze (kids, ages 12 and under): a small beef or chicken patty with cheese, served with small fries and a juice box. Price: $6.90.
