# Allergens and Dietary Information

Our kitchens handle wheat (gluten), milk, eggs, soy, sesame, mustard and peanuts. We cannot guarantee that any item is completely free of allergens because of shared preparation areas and fryers.

Brioche buns contain wheat, milk, eggs and sesame. Gluten-free buns are available for $1.50 extra, but they are toasted on a shared grill.

Blaze sauce contains egg and mustard. Chipotle mayo contains egg. Herb yoghurt contains milk.

All fries, onion rings and the Crispy Chicken Flame are cooked in the same fryer, which uses sunflower oil.

Halal: our beef and chicken are sourced from halal-certified suppliers, but our outlets are not halal-certified because we serve bacon.

Customers with severe allergies should speak to the outlet manager before ordering. If you have an allergic reaction after eating our food, seek medical help immediately and then contact our support team.
