BLAZE REWARDS LOYALTY PROGRAM

Join Blaze Rewards for free in the Blaze Burger Co. app. You earn 1 point for every $1 spent, in store or in the app.

Rewards:
100 points = free Classic Fries (regular)
250 points = free Classic Blaze burger
400 points = free burger meal (any burger with regular fries and a soft drink)

Points expire 12 months after they are earned. Points cannot be exchanged for cash.

Birthday treat: members get a free milkshake during their birthday month. The birthday must be added to the app profile at least 30 days in advance.

CURRENT PROMOTIONS

Tuesday Two-for-One: every Tuesday, buy one Classic Blaze and get a second Classic Blaze free. Dine-in and takeaway only, not valid on delivery.

Student deal: show a valid student card for 10% off any order on weekdays before 17:00.

Promotions cannot be combined with each other. Only one promotion can be applied per order.
