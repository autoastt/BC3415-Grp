# Ordering, Delivery and Refund Policy

## How to order
Order at the counter, through the Blaze Burger Co. app (iOS and Android), or on our website. Delivery is available from the Jurong Point outlet within a 5 km radius.

Delivery fee: $3.50. Free delivery on orders of $35 or more. Minimum delivery order: $15. Estimated delivery time is 30 to 45 minutes.

Payment: we accept Visa, Mastercard, PayNow, Apple Pay, Google Pay and cash (cash at the counter only).

## Cancelling an order
App and website orders can be cancelled for a full refund within 5 minutes of placing them, or any time before the kitchen starts preparing them. After preparation starts, orders cannot be cancelled.

## Refund policy
If your order is missing items, incorrect, or of unsatisfactory quality, contact us within 24 hours of receiving it. Please include your order number and a photo of the issue.

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. For cash payments, we offer an in-store refund or store credit.

If a delivery arrives more than 30 minutes later than the estimated time, you can request a refund of the delivery fee.

We do not offer refunds for change of mind after food has been prepared.

Refund requests are reviewed by our support team. The chat assistant cannot approve or process refunds itself.
