# Opening Hours and Locations

## Jurong Point outlet
Address: 1 Jurong West Central 2, #02-15, Singapore 648886.
Hours: Monday to Thursday 11:00 to 22:00. Friday and Saturday 11:00 to 23:00. Sunday 11:00 to 21:00.
Dine-in, takeaway and delivery.

## Tanjong Pagar outlet
Address: 25 Tanjong Pagar Road, Singapore 088451.
Hours: Monday to Friday 10:30 to 22:00. Saturday and Sunday 12:00 to 22:00.
Dine-in and takeaway only. No delivery from this outlet.

## Public holidays
All outlets open on public holidays with Sunday hours. All outlets close at 18:00 on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve, and are closed on the first day of Chinese New Year.

## Last orders
Kitchens take last orders 30 minutes before closing.
