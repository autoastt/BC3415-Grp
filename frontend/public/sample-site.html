Allergen and dietary guide

Our kitchens handle wheat (gluten), milk, eggs, soy, sesame, mustard and peanuts. Because of shared preparation areas and fryers, we can't guarantee any item is allergen-free.

ItemContains
Brioche bunWheat, milk, eggs, sesame
Blaze sauceEgg, mustard
Chipotle mayoEgg
Herb yoghurtMilk

Gluten-free buns cost $1.50 extra and are toasted on a shared grill. Fries, onion rings and the Crispy Chicken Flame share one sunflower-oil fryer. The Garden Ember can be made vegan at no extra cost.

Halal: beef and chicken come from halal-certified suppliers, but our outlets aren't halal-certified because we serve bacon.

Severe allergy? Speak to the outlet manager before ordering. If you have a reaction, get medical help first, then contact support.
